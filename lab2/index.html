Lab 2: Multivariate Visualization with D3

I use a combined way of bar chart and bubble chart to visualize the multivariate data. Bar height represents the population, since position and length make quantitative comparisons easy. Temperature is represented by the vertical position of the bubble, allowing viewers to compare temperatures across cities. The bubble size represents the development level, where larger bubbles represent higher development levels. Region is represented by bubble color, a categorical color scale is selected so that cities from the same region can be easily identified. The city names are shown along the x-axis, while tooltips provide the exact values for each city. This design allows viewers to compare population and temperature simultaneously while also identifying patterns related to development level and geographic region.
